Heal, Blur or Crop? Choosing a Watermark Removal Method

The best method depends on the watermark itself: where it sits, how opaque it is, and whether it moves.

Heal (delogo)

The delogo filter interpolates surrounding pixels across the marked area. It produces the most natural result on static, semi-transparent logos in quiet backgrounds. On busy textures or moving marks it can smear.

Blur

Blur replaces the area with a softened copy of itself. It never invents detail, so it is the safest choice for moving watermarks, busy backgrounds and opaque logos. The trade-off is a visible blurred patch.

Crop

Crop cuts the marked edges off the frame entirely. If a watermark hugs a corner or an edge and you can spare the picture, this is the only method that leaves zero traces. If the region is not near an edge, the tool falls back to heal automatically.

A quick rule of thumb

Static corner logo → heal. Moving or opaque mark → blur. Edge watermark and spare pixels → crop.
