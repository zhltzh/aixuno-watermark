Copyright and Watermark Removal: The Rules We Follow

Watermarks often exist to signal ownership. That is why Aixuno Watermark is built around a simple rule: only videos you own or have explicit rights to edit.

What is allowed

Your own exports, screen recordings, client footage you delivered or are licensed to edit, and personal home videos. You confirm this with the ownership checkbox before each job.

What is not allowed

Removing watermarks, logos or attribution from third-party content you do not own — including downloading videos from platforms to strip their marks. We do not offer link extraction for this reason.

Why it matters

Removing copyright management information can be illegal in many jurisdictions and unfair to creators. Keeping the tool ownership-first protects you and protects the people who made the original work.

If you are unsure

Ask the rights holder for a clean master or written permission. That is always safer than removing a watermark.
