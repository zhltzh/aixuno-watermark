Box Selection Tips for Cleaner Watermark Removal

The boxes you draw decide which pixels get processed. A little care here improves every method.

Fit the watermark, not the whole corner

Smaller boxes mean more original detail survives, especially for heal and crop. Draw just around the mark with a small margin.

Use several boxes instead of one big one

If there are two or three marks, mark them separately. Each box gets its own filter chain, which is cleaner than processing a large empty area between marks.

Check the frame before submitting

We use a frame near the start of the video. If a watermark fades in later, seek a frame where it is visible so your boxes cover it fully.

Common mistakes

Boxes that are too large (heal smears), boxes that miss the mark's edges (visible leftovers), and forgetting the ownership confirmation checkbox.
